ABC Technologies Pvt Ltd
Employee Leave & Attendance Policy
1. Working Hours

Employees are expected to work 9 hours per day, including a 1-hour lunch break.

Office Timings:

Start Time: 9:00 AM
End Time: 6:00 PM

Employees must mark attendance through the company attendance system.

2. Casual Leave (CL)

Every employee is entitled to:

12 Casual Leaves per calendar year
Unused Casual Leaves cannot be carried forward
Casual Leave cannot be encashed

Employees should apply for leave at least one day in advance whenever possible.

3. Sick Leave (SL)

Employees are entitled to:

8 Sick Leaves per year

Rules:

Medical certificate required if leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.
Unused Sick Leaves expire at the end of the year.
4. Earned Leave (EL)

Employees receive:

18 Earned Leaves per year

Rules:

Can be carried forward up to 45 days.
Balance beyond 45 days will lapse.
Can be encashed during resignation according to company policy.
5. Work From Home Policy

Employees may work from home under the following conditions:

Manager approval is mandatory.
Maximum 8 WFH days per month.
Employees must remain available during business hours.
6. Late Coming Policy

Grace period:

15 minutes per day.

Rules:

More than 3 late arrivals in a month will result in half-day leave deduction.
Repeated violations may lead to disciplinary action.
7. Public Holidays

Employees are eligible for company-declared public holidays.

Typical holidays include:

New Year's Day
Republic Day
Independence Day
Gandhi Jayanti
Christmas

The company holiday calendar will be published annually.

8. Maternity Leave

Eligible female employees are entitled to:

26 weeks of paid maternity leave

As per applicable laws.

9. Paternity Leave

Eligible male employees are entitled to:

10 working days of paid paternity leave

To be utilized within six months of childbirth.

10. Travel Reimbursement Policy

Employees traveling for official business are eligible for reimbursement.

Eligible expenses:

Airfare
Train fare
Taxi charges
Hotel accommodation
Meals

Requirements:

Original bills must be submitted.
Claims must be filed within 15 days of travel completion.
11. Internet Reimbursement

Employees working remotely may claim:

Up to ₹1,500 per month for internet expenses.

Submission of a valid bill is mandatory.

12. Performance Review

Performance reviews are conducted:

Twice every year

Review months:

June
December

Factors considered:

Technical skills
Productivity
Team collaboration
Customer feedback
13. Resignation Notice Period

Notice period requirements:

Employee Level	Notice Period
Associate	30 Days
Senior Associate	60 Days
Manager	90 Days

The company may waive notice period requirements at its discretion.

14. Employee Benefits

Employees are eligible for:

Health Insurance Coverage of ₹5,00,000
Group Term Life Insurance
Annual Health Checkup
Employee Assistance Program
15. Code of Conduct

Employees must:

Maintain professional behavior.
Protect company confidential information.
Avoid workplace harassment.
Follow company security guidelines.

Violation may result in disciplinary action, including termination.
